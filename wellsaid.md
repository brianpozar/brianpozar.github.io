WellSaid is an iOS app and set of Lock Screen/Home Screen widgets that surface short, high‑signal bits of content—quotes, words, facts, sayings, rules, motivational lines, and translations—throughout your day. Content is backed by a structured Google Sheet, so you can start with a curated default set or plug in your own sheet when you’re ready to customize.

Instead of doom‑scrolling, you get small, intentional doses of language and ideas: a single item on the Today screen, widgets that refresh on a schedule you control, and optional notifications at times you choose.

## What you can do with WellSaid

- **See something interesting every time you check your phone:** Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets show a rotating mix of content pulled from your enabled categories.
- **Pick the kinds of content you want:** Turn categories on and off (Words, Quotes, Sayings, Facts, Rules, Motivation, Translations) and fine‑tune edgy or humorous content with NSFW and Funny filters.
- **Go deeper than a single card:** Tap into the app to read full definitions, sample sentences, sources, and translations, then swipe up or down to move through the rotation without repeats.
- **Save and study favorites:** Star items you like, revisit them in the Favorites tab, and (with Premium) run them as simple flashcards when you want a more focused review session.
- **Use your own content:** Add new items directly in the app (for any supported type), or—if you want to go further—use the built‑in data tools (Premium) to import/export CSV files and maintain larger collections outside the app.
- **Bring your own Google Sheet:** Advanced users can point WellSaid at their own publicly readable Google Sheet. The app includes guided help for sheet structure, tags, and the `IsActive` flag so you can control exactly what appears.
- **Stay offline‑friendly:** Content is cached on‑device and shared with the widget via an App Group, so widgets and the app continue to work even when you’re offline.
- **Stay in control of notifications:** Turn daily notifications on or off, schedule multiple times, and choose what category each notification should use—or let WellSaid pick randomly from your enabled categories.

## Privacy

WellSaid is built to be privacy‑first. It does not collect personal data or send your content to external servers; everything stays on your device and in your own iCloud account if you have iCloud enabled.
