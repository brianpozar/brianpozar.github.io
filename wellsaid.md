WellSaid is an iOS app designed to help you learn something new every day. It draws from a curated collection of words, quotes, and sayings built up over more than 20 years, then surfaces them at a comfortable pace through Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets, with CarPlay support planned. You choose how often you want to see something new and WellSaid handles the rest, rotating content without repeats and using gentle, user‑configurable notification schedules so vocabulary and insights build naturally over time.

## Key features

- **Daily learning, on your terms:** Set how frequently you want to see something new and let the app do the rest.
- **Curated over decades:** Content comes from more than 20 years of collected words, quotes, and ideas.
- **Widgets everywhere:** Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets make it easy to catch something new in passing, with CarPlay widgets planned.
- **Works offline:** Content is cached on-device so you still see items even without a network connection.
- **Privacy-first:** No accounts, no analytics, and no ads. Your preferences stay on your device.

## Privacy

WellSaid is built to be privacy-first. It does not collect personal data or send your content to external servers.
