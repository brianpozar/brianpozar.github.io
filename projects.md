Below are a few of the apps I’ve worked on. The goal here is to give a quick sense of what they do and who they’re for, not a full technical deep dive.

### TruerWords

TruerWords is an iOS app that helps you learn something new every day with carefully curated words, quotes, and ideas from 20+ years of notes—delivered through widgets and gentle, user‑controlled notifications.

- **Platform:** iOS (with Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets; CarPlay planned)
- **Focus:** Expanding vocabulary and knowledge in small, daily moments
- **Status:** Active project

### FlightGrade

FlightGrade is an iPhone and iPad app for flight instructors that replaces after‑lesson paperwork with a clear, structured view of each student’s training program. After every flight or ground lesson you can quickly record what was covered, capture notes, and update progress so you and your student always know what’s next.

- **Platform:** iOS and iPadOS (iPhone and iPad, including iPad mini)
- **Focus:** Helping CFIs track student progress across training programs with simple progress bars, ratings, and notes
- **Status:** Active project (early stages)

FlightGrade is designed to work offline during lessons and then sync updates to iCloud when you’re back online, so your records stay backed up and consistent across devices.
